# E0021: EXIT, CONTINUE or JMP where it cannot go

`EXIT` leaves, and `CONTINUE` restarts, the innermost `FOR`, `WHILE` or
`REPEAT` (Ed.3 7.3.3.4); outside one there is nothing for them to act
on. To leave a POU, use `RETURN`.

With [jump-labels](../extensions.md#jump-labels-jmp-and-labels-in-st-as-codesys-and-twincat-write-them-never-into-a-loop-or-branch),
a `JMP` goes to a label of its own statement list or of one around it,
in the same body: out of a loop or an IF, never into one it is not in.

```
FOR k := 1 TO 3 DO
  inner: ;
END_FOR;
JMP inner;        (* E0021: into the FOR *)
```
