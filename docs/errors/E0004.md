# E0004: a literal that is not well formed

Numeric, duration, date and direct-address literals have fixed shapes
(Ed.3 6.3, Tables 5, 8 and 9, and 6.5.5 for `%` addresses). Examples
that are refused:

```
x := 3#12;         (* bases are 2, 8 and 16 *)
x := 16#;          (* no digits *)
t := T#;           (* no duration *)
a AT %IY0 : BOOL;  (* areas are I, Q and M; sizes X, B, W, D and L *)
x := 1__000;       (* an underscore stands between two digits *)
x := 16#FF_;
t := T#1_s;        (* in a duration, between two digits or after a unit *)
```

An underscore in a number is single and stands between two digits:
"No other use of underscore characters in numeric literals is allowed"
(Ed.3 6.3.2). After a base's `#` one may come before the first digit,
`16#_FF` (Annex A, Hex_Int), and in a duration one may follow a unit,
`T#1h_30m` (Table 8).
